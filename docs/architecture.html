AI-driven chaos engineering: architecture

An AI layer plans and judges the experiments. Chaos Mesh or LitmusChaos does the breaking, and OpenTelemetry provides the evidence. Everything runs in a local sandbox cluster.

Safety abort path Fix-and-verify loop

How a run flows

  1. 1
    Select and understand the app. The mapper reads the repo's manifests and builds a service dependency graph.
  2. 2
    Plan experiments. The LLM ranks candidate failures from a fixed allowlist by how informative or risky they are, and records its reasoning.
  3. 3
    Inject controlled failures. The chaos engine applies the chosen fault to one service. The safety guard watches live metrics and triggers an abort if limits are crossed.
  4. 4
    Observe behavior. OpenTelemetry feeds Prometheus and Jaeger with errors, slowdowns and recovery time.
  5. 5
    Analyze and report. The analyzer scores resilience, explains weak points and suggests fixes. After a fix is applied, the same experiment re-runs and the score is compared.
